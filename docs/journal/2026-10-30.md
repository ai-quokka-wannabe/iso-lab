By the registered meaning the cold-band hunch is refuted for this rule too: N1 fails. N2 holding does not rescue it,
for the contrast comes from harm in the warm band, which is a cost, not the cold-band help the hunch claimed. What
the two nights do show, descriptively and from one body seed per layout: in these fresh banded worlds, steering by
the map's global contrast without the margin harms the gated worm where the band is warm, and the greedy rule's
margin limits that harm. Neither version is shown to help over greedy gating anywhere in these ten layouts, and both
mostly end below never steering. The global-contrast fix, designed on 75000, does not generalise as a fix; it stays
safe in rich worlds (§ The Margin Rule in Five Fresh Rich Layouts, 2026-10-29), and that is all it has shown beyond
the layouts it was designed on.
