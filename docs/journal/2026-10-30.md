## The Global Rule Without the Margin on Ten Fresh Layouts

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

STATE.md item 9(b) leaves one arm of the cold-band test unrun: the global rule without the greedy rule's 0.02
margin. The global rule steers towards the side of the map's peak; the margin rule adds that requirement. Last night
the margin rule's cold-band lead over greedy gating was +0.032, and the hunch was refuted narrowly (journal of
2026-10-29, § The Cold-Band Hunch on Ten Fresh Layouts). Without the margin, the rule lifted the gated worm by 0.167
in 75000 and not in 23001 (journal of 2026-10-28, § Steering by the Map's Global Contrast). This asks the same
questions of it on the ten fresh banded layouts.

- **The worlds and the comparison arms**: the ten banded layouts of 2026-10-26, cold 82000 to 82004 and warm 82100 to
  82104, one body seed each, L + 777. The greedy gated and never-steering arms are last night's lives in exactly these
  worlds and body seeds (`fresh10/glob.json` `:greedy` keys and `fresh10/ctl.json`), not re-run. The code is
  deterministic given the seeds, and last night's control F0 showed `glob4.py`'s `:greedy` arm repeating `gate.py`
  bit for bit. The margin rule's lives from the same file are compared descriptively.
- **The new arm**: `glob.py` (SHA-256 `b41a31bd849d1db6ec48b955262294892a01a52cb54fdd7a9f9809f4fa961efe`, as
  registered on 2026-10-28), which differs from `glob4.py` only in lacking the margin test, run on `L:side:L+777`.
  100 worms, 48 h, late energy over h24-48, the fixed body, gr-fec's code unedited.
- **Commands**, from the repository's root:

By the registered meaning the cold-band hunch is refuted for this rule too: N1 fails. N2 holding does not rescue it,
for the contrast comes from harm in the warm band, which is a cost, not the cold-band help the hunch claimed. What
the two nights do show, descriptively and from one body seed per layout: in these fresh banded worlds, steering by
the map's global contrast without the margin harms the gated worm where the band is warm, and the greedy rule's
margin limits that harm. Neither version is shown to help over greedy gating as a band. Layout by layout, the margin
rule's lead was wholly above zero in two cold layouts (82001 and 82004, journal of 2026-10-29) and the no-margin
rule's in none. Both mostly end below never steering. The global-contrast fix, designed on 75000, does not generalise as a fix; it stays
safe in rich worlds (§ The Margin Rule in Five Fresh Rich Layouts, 2026-10-29), and that is all it has shown beyond
the layouts it was designed on.
