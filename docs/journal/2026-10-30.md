## H14: Graded Slow Knobs, Change-Detecting Senses and Individuality

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer.

The maintainer brought a picture of the finished worm brain from a conversation with another Claude session, marked
there as a hunch: a few pages of equations, five layers (body-driven undulation, senses reporting change,
pirouettes and weathervaning, slow neuromodulatory state, a few slow learned numbers), with individuality and
noise as the part people underestimate. The same conversation reminded the lab of its own position: no creature is
scored, Phase 0 is not frozen, and the toy-worm results are evidence about method, not about *C. elegans*. That is
true and is what STATE.md and findings.md already say.

Most of the picture is already in the hypotheses: H2 (states), H3 (slow internal state), H4 (rhythm through the
body), H6 (small learning) and H12 (a plain worm brain). Copying it would duplicate them, so it was added as H14,
holding only what is new: slow state as a graded knob rather than a switch, senses that report change, a remembered
set point instead of a map, and persistent individuality.

The three sources new to the lab were read at their abstracts through Europe PMC:

- [Suzuki et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06927): ASE's ON and OFF cells report changes in
  concentration;
- [Stern et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.10.041): persistent non-genetic individuality in
  isogenic worms;
- [Hedgecock and Russell 1975](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.72.10.4061): worms migrate to their growth temperature,
  added after verification.

Their full texts are not yet read, and H14 says so. The claim that neuromodulators
act as graded gains on turning has no source yet, and H14 says that too.

H14 makes one prediction the toy worm can test now. A hunger *gain* on steering, steering scaled by hunger, should do
better across fresh layouts than the on/off hunger gate whose harm findings.md § D4 traces. The two nights of the
global-contrast fix failing on fresh layouts (§ The Global Rule Without the Margin on Ten Fresh Layouts, above, and
the journal of 2026-10-29) make that the natural next test of the gate, and it goes on STATE.md item 9.

## H14's Graded Hunger Gain on Ten Fresh Layouts

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

H14 ([#160](https://github.com/ai-quokka-wannabe/iso-lab/pull/160), *a hunch*) says slow state should act as a graded
knob, not a switch. On the toy worm that predicts a hunger *gain* on steering should do better than the on/off
hunger gate whose harm findings.md § D4 traces. This tests that prediction on the ten fresh banded layouts, where
the on/off gate and both global-contrast variants mostly ended below never steering.

- **The brain**: gr-fec's greedy SteeringBrain, as `steer/ctl.py` builds it. Its raw steering command, set by the
  greedy rule every 10 s, is multiplied each second by the brain's own hunger reading (sense `y[7]`, clipped to
  [0, 1]), instead of being switched off below 0.3. The raw command is kept apart, so the scaling does not compound
  between decisions. The gain has no free parameter, and it reads only a sense the brain already has.
- **The worlds and the comparison arms**: the ten banded layouts of 2026-10-26, one body seed each, L + 777. The greedy
  gated and never-steering arms are the lives of 2026-10-29 in exactly these worlds and seeds (`fresh10/glob.json`
  `:greedy`, `fresh10/ctl.json` `fixed:plain`), not re-run.
- **Scripts**:
    - `graded.py` (SHA-256 `50799e7b2e9338dfc272a839eb8676ad80e77d6f5d37bd8d13202cfab20c375b`), new;
    - `gradstats.py` (`b6709ca1265765dd7798e2aa9519df00aafe441b7b342ec59a52431ba6efdfdf`), new;
    - `run.sh` (`cd4f754aad40b32b4550211e2919ec232b82e64d31d7ccfb689fd523276eefb4`), which runs `graded.py` on the ten
      jobs `L:side:L+777` and the control `82000:-1:82777:flat`, then `steer/ctl.py` on `fixed:greedy:82000:-1:82777`.
    - Then `python3 -I <s>/graded/gradstats.py <s>/graded/graded.json <s>/fresh10/glob.json <s>/fresh10/ctl.json 99960`.
    - 100 worms, 48 h, late energy over h24-48, the fixed body, gr-fec's code unedited.
- **Rules**:
    - **C0**: with the gain held at 1 (`:flat`), `graded.py`'s life in 82000 equals `ctl.py`'s ungated greedy life
      there, every worm's late energy (`np.array_equal`).
    - **K1** (H14's prediction): over all ten layouts, the mean over layouts of graded minus greedy gated is above
      zero, with the two-stage interval's lower bound above zero.
    - Descriptive: **K2**, the same against never steering; each band's means; each layout's differences.
- **What each outcome would mean**:
    - K1 holding: a graded hunger gain beats the on/off gate on fresh layouts, as H14 predicts for the toy worm.
    - K1 failing: that prediction of H14 is refuted for the toy worm.
    - C0 failing invalidates the comparison until explained.
    - One body seed per layout: a failure to show is not a showing of no effect.

beats the on/off gate across the ten fresh banded layouts, where the global-contrast fix did not (§ The Global Rule

beat it on fresh layouts. The two arms differ in two ways, not one, as the verifier of
[#161](https://github.com/ai-quokka-wannabe/iso-lab/pull/161) pointed out:

- the gain is graded, not on or off;
- it is not latched. `gate.py` zeroes the command whenever the reading falls below 0.3, and the zero stays until the
  next 10-s decision even if hunger rises again, while the graded brain rescales from the raw command every second.

Which of the two carries the lead is not separated here. The ablation that separates them, an un-latched on/off gain
(`raw` times one if the reading is at least 0.3, else zero), goes on STATE.md's *Next*. It does not undo the gate's
harm: against a worm that never steers, the
graded worm is not shown better overall, and in the warm band it is still worse. One body seed per layout, and the
comparison arms are last night's lives; the interval's lower bound, +0.0046, is close to zero.
