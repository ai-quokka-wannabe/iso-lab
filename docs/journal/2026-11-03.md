**Verdict, by the rule at the head of [B.md](../hypotheses/experiments/B.md): C18 is refuted**, by clauses (2), (4)
and (5), each at alpha 0.05 / 6. Its first half stands. The pure network has a cliff, a reach that stays finite below
d = 2.255 and becomes a share of the network above it, and two-hop priming is well below one-hop. Its cures do not
stand as cures. Lateral inhibition and the fan-out penalty move the cliff to higher connectivity rather than remove
it. So does the conserved budget at a gain above one, to lower connectivity. The one variant without a cliff, the
budget at a gain below one, also spreads nothing to speak of.

**Against the expectations registered on 2026-10-31:**

- *The pure network reads cliff near d = 2.5*: held. D is 2.255. The 1% crossing is interpolated from below the first
  grid point past 1%, 2.50, which is also where the coefficient of variation peaks.
- *The budget at c = 0.2 never passes 1%, so it has no cliff and no spread*: held. Its highest mean is 0.00023.
- *The budget at c = 0.6 reads cliff, firing clause (4)*: held. *Near d = 1, the lead's topological transition*: did
  not hold. D is 1.528, and the coefficient of variation peaks at 1.75, not at 1. Why is not settled here. A
  candidate, *a hunch* not tested: with a budget, a unit of out-degree k passes 0.6 / k of its activation to each
  target each step, so at the fixed point a target with that one input holds 1.2 / k of it. A target with one active
  input is then activated only when k is small, and the activation threshold of 0.1 raises the effective percolation
  point above the graph's own, which is 1. The lead's region III is likewise defined by units "above a given positive
  threshold" (journal of 2026-10-31), but in an unbounded linear model whose activity grows without end, where any
  threshold is crossed in time.
- *The fan-out penalty and inhibition read cliff at higher degrees, firing (5) and (2)*: held, at 7.502 and 4.753.
- *If that is how it comes out, the gain, not the budget, decides*: as far as these five networks go, yes. The budget
  removes the cliff only at a gain where nothing spreads; at a gain where it spreads, it has a cliff.
- *Clause (6)* had no registered expectation. Its ratio, 0.400, is what a linear reading predicts. Below the
  threshold, near the seed at N = 10 000, the graph is a tree, so a unit one hop out holds 0.4 at the fixed point and
  one two hops out 0.4 squared, 0.16. The clause tests the arithmetic of the edge weight more than any property of the
  spread.

## Why Steering Trails Never Steering in Warm Layouts

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

STATE.md item 9 (b) leaves one question open: why gated steering of any kind still trails never steering in warm
layouts. Over the five warm layouts of 2026-10-26 (82100 to 82104), every gated arm so far ended below never steering.
The no-margin global rule was below in all five, each interval wholly below zero. The graded gain's warm band is
−0.0490 (−0.1087 to −0.0041) (journal of 2026-10-30, § The Global Rule Without the Margin on Ten Fresh Layouts and
§ H14's Graded Hunger Gain on Ten Fresh Layouts). In the cold band the same arms are mixed. A toy world: none of
this is evidence about *C. elegans*.

**The question**: in warm layouts, what part of the worm's steering costs it energy, and is warmth what matters?

**A reading of the code, done before the run.** Is the toy world the same on both sides? I traced gr-fec's code
(`reference/gr-fec/src/embodied.py`, `lifeworld.py`, `steer.py`), and it appears to be mirror-symmetric: an equivariant
system. Mirror a layout in x and every temperature t becomes 40 − t. The brain's temperature prototypes have a fixed
width, so they map onto mirrored ones. Its "colder" and "warmer" neighbours swap, and so do the left and right lateral
readings. A left turn becomes a right turn. The noise is symmetric, and so is the worms' starting heading. Steering
costs no energy and does not change speed: `DirBody.act` only rotates the heading by 0.15 rad a second, and
`Body.metabolise` never reads the steering command. If this reading is right, a "warm" layout and its mirror image,
a "cold" layout with the same geometry, give the same lives in distribution, though not worm for worm. The warm band's
harm would then belong to those five layouts, not to warmth. The reading is mine and unchecked by any run; the mirror
arms below check it.

**Candidate mechanisms**, each *a hunch*:

- **A, an immature map.** Steering on the first day's map leaves the worm worse placed, or its food worse depleted, by
  hour 24. Refuted if steering only from hour 24 (`Gl`) does not beat steering throughout (`G`): A's interval must
  lie wholly above zero.
- **B, steering pulls the worm off the band.** The greedy rule compares only the current concept's two neighbours,
  never the current concept itself, so a worm on the band can be sent off it. In a warm layout, colder means towards
  the gap and the smelly food. Refuted unless the cold-ward turns alone (`Gc`) cost against never steering, with the
  interval wholly below zero, while the warm-ward turns alone (`Gw`) do not.
- **C, steering overshoots the band to the plate's edge.** The band sits at x = 35 ± 6 on a plate of radius 50, and a
  10-s decision turns the head by up to 1.5 rad. Refuted unless the warm-ward turns alone (`Gw`) cost against never
  steering, wholly below zero, while the cold-ward turns alone (`Gc`) do not. The share of time beyond the band (x above
  41) is a descriptive check.
- **D, not warmth but the layouts** (from the reading above). Refuted if the mirrored layouts' harm differs from the
  warm layouts', the interval of (mG − mN) − (G − N) excluding zero.

**The design.**

- **The brain**: H14's graded hunger gain (`graded/graded.py`'s `GradedBrain`, imported unchanged), the best gated
  steering so far.
- **Each mechanism is switched off in turn**, from the brain's own signals only. Direction is judged by its own lateral
  sense at the decision and the sign of its own command; time by its own tick counter. Nothing from the world reaches
  the brain:
    - `N`: never steers;
    - `G`: the graded gain;
    - `Gw`: decisions that would turn towards colder are set to straight;
    - `Gc`: decisions that would turn towards warmer are set to straight;
    - `Gl`: every decision before hour 24 is set to straight;
    - `mN`, `mG`: `N` and `G` in the layout mirrored in x. Food band, smelly food, decoys and salt pans all go to −x,
      which makes a cold layout with the same geometry.
- **Layouts and seeds**, fresh, chosen now: warm Hardland layouts 82500 to 82504 (`RandomHardWorld`, side +1), body
  seed L + 777 (83277 to 83281). No earlier run has used these layout seeds. 100 worms a life, 48 h, the fixed body,
  late energy over h24-48 as before. Measured over h24-48 for evaluation only: the share of time on band food, on
  smelly food and beyond the band; steering time; the decisions turning warm-ward and cold-ward; energy at h24.
- **Scripts**, in the scratchpad `<s>` like every toy-worm script (gr-fec.md § 11), to be published in the pull
  request's body:
    - `warm/warm.py`, SHA-256 `055d0286227c257ef0396e10fb1195a8c830b08b653acc1df3543a460bb647b8`;
    - `warm/warmstats.py`, `f5b80c86f55a5066e577d84d3fee4a54ae494659f6f81af8bbd1c74c2854154b`;
    - `warm/run.sh`, `7b34da6c3fcaf6f96e0b4b215820800a00a5bbd5a6279d70679809e790c82349`.
- **Commands**, from the repository's root:
    - `nohup bash <s>/warm/run.sh &`. It runs `warm.py` with two processes on 36 lives, in this order: the control,
      then `N`, `G`, `Gw`, `Gc`, `mN`, `mG` and `Gl`, each over the five layouts.
    - Then `python3 -I <s>/warm/warmstats.py <s> 99801`.
    - Python 3.13.16, numpy 2.5.4.
- **The cut-off**: if any lives are unfinished at 23:05 UTC, they are stopped and reported as not run. Their tests are
  then not read.
- **Control C0**: `G` at `82100:82877` must equal `graded/graded.json`'s life `82100:1:82877` worm for worm
  (`np.array_equal`). Its red-first twin compares the same life with the never-steering life there and must print
  False. If C0 fails, the run is void until explained.

**Statistics**, as every toy-worm comparison since 2026-10-28: a mean over the five layouts of each per-layout
difference of arm means, with a 95% percentile interval from a two-stage bootstrap (layouts, then worms within each
arm), 10 000 resamples, seed 99801.

**Decision rules.**

- **P0, the harm reproduced**: G − N wholly below zero. If P0 fails, the fresh layouts do not show the harm, and A to
  C are reported but read as uninformative.
- **A**: Gl − G wholly above zero supports A; otherwise A is refuted.
- **B and C**: Gc − N and Gw − N, each by the rule in its candidate above. If both are wholly below zero, each
  direction costs alone, and neither B nor C is singled out. If neither is, the harm is not carried by one direction
  alone.
- **D**: if mG − mN is wholly below zero and the interval of (mG − mN) − (G − N) includes zero, D is supported: the
  harm goes with the geometry, not the warmth. If that interval excludes zero, D is refuted. Including zero bounds the
  difference; it does not show that the difference is zero.
- **Descriptive**: Gl − N, the additivity of the two directions ((Gw − N) + (Gc − N) − (G − N)), the diagnostics'
  differences and every layout's figures. Five layouts, one body seed each. A failure to show is not a showing of no
  effect.
