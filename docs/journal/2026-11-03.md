      CHANGELOG says no simulated bit, golden or wire byte moved.
    - *The Grid*: BYE from Master Control is a clean end, not a fatal error
      ([tron-grid-lite#176](https://github.com/ai-quokka-wannabe/tron-grid-lite/pull/176)); a misspelt flag, or one

      `--list-programs` names a library without the Program entry point as "not a Program"

      build; tests, documents and `docs/PROTOCOL.md`. No byte of the wire and no client ABI moved, by its CHANGELOG.

- **Rules**:

      its sources change, so the build stamp is expected to differ.
    - **D2**: the three device checks and `ctest` pass at both pins; the Grid's tests change, so the counts may differ

    - **D4** (descriptive): the placeholder's 320-tick life and Clu's replay at each pin. Following the lesson of
      2026-10-31, refused intents are counted in every life first (`grep -c refused_stale life.log`), and a life with

  `git submodule status --recursive`, runs `arena.sh`, then `life.sh` up to five times until a life has no
  `refused_stale` verdict. Each from an empty Grid build directory, one after the other with nothing else running:
  old pins from 19:55:52 to 20:01:59 UTC, new pins from 20:03:17 to 20:06:03. The recursive status recorded
  tron-grid-lite `328673e`, master-control `53e5acb` and link `9b1b771` at the old pins; `ba76cdd`, `28bda1c` and
  `5c63e87` at the new; and the Link nested in each of tron-grid-lite and master-control at `9b1b771` at both. The lab's

  before it had built anything, because `git submodule update` had put the old pins back before the new ones were
  staged; the run reported here was started afresh after staging them.
