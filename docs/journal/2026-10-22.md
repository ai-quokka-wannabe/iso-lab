## H13: A Tap Protocol That Tells Depression from Recovery?

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent, at the maintainer's request; not by the maintainer. Written before it
runs.

The profile of 2026-10-19 (journal, § H13: Does the Rest Protocol Sharpen the Valley at All?) found the statistics
nearly blind to the trade between habituation's depression, `dep`, and its recovery time, `tau`. Along the trade the
distance rises one to two hundred times less than across it, under both the old protocol and the rest protocol.

*A hunch*: taps at two different intervals give two different habituated levels, and two levels fix two parameters
where one cannot. The real assay's habituation depends on the interval between taps ([Rankin and Broster
1992](https://doi.org/10.1037/0735-7044.106.2.239), abstract). This tests that on the toy, in two stages fixed here.

- **The protocols**, each within the 600 s recording, taps from 300 s:
    - **old**: 30 taps at 10 s; bins of taps 0-3, 3-10, 10-20 and 20-30.
    - **rest**: 20 taps at 10 s, then probes at 520, 550 and 580 s; bins 0-3, 3-10, 10-20 and each probe.
    - **twoisi**: 10 taps at 10 s (300 to 390 s), then 6 at 30 s (420 to 570 s); bins 0-3, 3-10, 10-12, 12-14 and
      14-16.
    - **slowfirst**: 6 taps at 30 s (300 to 450 s), then 14 at 10 s (460 to 590 s); bins 0-2, 2-4, 4-6, 6-9, 9-14 and
      14-20.
    - **probe60**: 10 taps at 10 s, then probes at 450, 510 and 570 s; bins 0-3, 3-10 and each probe.
- **The script**: `h13_protocols.py` (SHA-256 `e8f280d883434e9fb1f6c1b24560ca3d82c37a0c132591d06e440ab361903bd6`)
  generalises the 2026-10-19 habituation script to a table of
  protocols. Its `check` mode reproduced the notebook bit for bit with the old taps, and failed with one bin moved,
  before this entry.
- **Stage 1, the profile**: `python3 -I h13_protocols.py profile <protocol>` for all five. It reports the trade cost
  (the mean distance over the anti-diagonal cells (1.4, 0.7), (1.2, 0.85), (0.85, 1.2) and (0.7, 1.4), less the
  truth's cell) at distance seeds 4343 to 4347, and the same for the diagonal, with each mean's 95% t interval.
    - **A built-in control**: old's and rest's trade costs must reproduce the 2026-10-19 values seed by seed
      (0.0003010 ... and 0.0005455 ... at seed 4343).
- **The selection rule**: a candidate (twoisi, slowfirst or probe60) qualifies if the lower end of its trade cost's
  interval exceeds five times old's mean trade cost. The winner is the qualifying candidate with the largest mean. If
  none qualifies, the hunch is **refuted** for designs that fit in 600 s, and stage 2 does not run.
- **Stage 2, the fit**: `python3 -I h13_protocols.py fit <winner> <7..14>`, on the winner's near noise-free target,
  with 128 worms per candidate, 360 generations and X pinned - the settings of the 2026-10-19 habituation test.
    - **H1**: `dep` and `tau` both within 0.2 (`|ln(fit / truth)| <= 0.2`) in at least 7 of 8 seeds. The old protocol
      gave 5 of 8 and the rest protocol 3 of 8.
    - **H2**: `k_rd` within 0.2 in at least 7 of 8.
- **What it would mean**: H1 holding would show the trade was the measurement's, and that two intervals cure it on
  the toy. The protocol would then go into the whole loop on fresh populations, pre-registered on its own. H1
  failing with a qualifying profile would mean a sharper valley is not yet enough at this fitter's budget.
