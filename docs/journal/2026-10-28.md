## The Composition Law Beyond Its First World and Pair

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

Last night's design rule (journal of 2026-10-27, § A First Design Rule) predicted a pair of gene settings from each
gene measured alone, by multiplying their remaining failures: E = EMAX − (EMAX − E₀₀)·a·b. It was found and tested on
one pair (slow learning `eta`, the smell instinct `g_odour`) in one world (the fresh cold layout), and at mild
settings the worm did better than the rule. This asks whether the rule holds elsewhere, and records two mechanisms in
every cell to look at why mild pairs beat it.

- **The sweeps**: `sweep2.py` (SHA-256 `d772cdec691c05a494696e11cf61c5065320876a99e725de35017101b9a9e2e4`), three
  4 × 4 grids on e45's median genome, rows and columns running from e45's value to the CMA-ES genome's, each cell a
  life exactly as `build.py` lives it (50 worms, body seeds + 777, 48 h, late energy over h24-48):
    - `cold_eo`: layout 75000 (cold), log10 `eta` {−2, −2.4, −2.8, −3.1623} × `g_odour` {1.7237, 1.2, 0.75, 0.3786} -
      last night's sweep, repeated;
    - `warm_eo`: layout 75001 (warm), the same grid. There the weak nose alone did little (journal of 2026-10-27,
      § Why Slow Learning and a Weak Nose Need Each Other);
    - `cold_et`: layout 75000, log10 `eta` as above × log10 `tau`, the turning rule's temperature, {−1.3542, −1.2,
      −1.07, −0.9368} - a learned-map lever composed with a turning-rule lever.

  Each cell also records, for evaluation only, the share of h24-48 near a decoy and the share of samples whose hungry
  temperature map values the food band above its mirror, as `probe.py` did. gr-fec's code is used unedited.
- **The test**: `law2.py` (SHA-256 `6f688b440b998aecacdeb048976187a3ebf04704e93e0cc216bbd2e694825b58`), `law.py`'s
  test unchanged - EMAX 0.90, the nine interior cells predicted from the edges by the OR law and by adding gains, mean
  absolute errors, bootstrap of worms within each cell, 10 000 resamples - with seeds 91000 (`warm_eo`) and 91001
  (`cold_et`). It also prints how far each lever alone moves the score.
- **Run**: `python3 -I <scratchpad>/sweep2/sweep2.py <sweep> <out.npz>` for each sweep in turn, then
  `python3 -I <scratchpad>/sweep2/law2.py <out.npz> <seed>`, from the repository's root.
- **Rules**:
    - **H0, the control**: `cold_eo`'s late energies equal last night's `sweep.npy` worm by worm.
    - **W1, the warm world**: the OR law's mean absolute error over the interior is at most 0.03.
    - **W2**: the OR law's error is below the additive rule's, with the interval of the difference below zero.
    - **T1, eta with tau in the cold world**: as W1.
    - **T2**: as W2.
    - If one lever alone moves the score by less than 0.02 along its whole edge, both rules then predict nearly the
      same, and W2 or T2 for that sweep is reported as uninformative, not as passed or failed.
    - **M1, a hunch about why mild pairs beat the law**: the weak nose also improves the map - with fewer visits to
      decoys, the learned values are less polluted. In `cold_eo`, in both mild rows (log10 `eta` −2.4 and −2.8), the
      share of samples whose map points to the food is higher in the weakest-nose column than in e45's (point
      estimates). It would be refuted if, in either row, it is not.
- **What each outcome would mean**: W1 and T1 holding make the rule a general first tool for composing levers in this
  worm, beyond the pair and world it was found in; failing marks where it stops. M1 holding offers a mechanism for the
  synergy - levers that repair one failure also ease the other - to be tested next; failing sets that one aside.

## Where Hungry Steering Points

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

Steering by learned value only while hungry left the worm below one that never steers in layouts 75000 and 23001, and
steering only while fed never did (journal of 2026-10-27, § Steering Only When Hungry, § A Hunger Gate with
Hysteresis, § Steering Only When Fed). The hungry worm's map values the food band above its mirror most of the time
(§ Why the Hunger Gate Harms), but gr-fec's greedy rule does not read that contrast: every 10 s it compares the
values of the two *neighbouring* temperature concepts at the worm's current hunger, and steers towards the better.
*A hunch*: at high hunger the hunger-gated weights give the value landscape a peak away from the food band, so hungry
steering climbs towards the wrong temperature.

- **The lives**: gr-fec's `SteeringBrain`, `greedy`, ungated, exactly as the positive control ran it (fixed body, 100
  worms, 48 h): layouts 75000 and 23000 (both cold, food band at 16.5 °C), body seeds + 777 and + 778. Recording
  draws no random numbers, so each life must repeat the control's greedy life.
- **The tool**: `dir.py` (SHA-256 `d6c5920b7a57f3915862f535ad758c27b848edd9d72b27d97ca3a47e1240539d`) classes every
  steering decision over h24-48 - the brain's 10-step update with a non-zero command - as towards colder or warmer
  temperature, from the command and the brain's own lateral reading exactly as gr-fec's rule maps them, and splits
  them by the worm's hunger at that moment (reading at or above 0.3: hungry). Every 600 s it records the temperature of
  the concept with the highest learned value at hunger 1 and at hunger 0.2. For evaluation only; nothing reaches the
  brain. Run as `python3 -I <scratchpad>/dir/dir.py <out.npz> <layout:side:body seed> ...` from the repository's root.
- **Statistics**: `dirstats.py` (SHA-256 `fedb9e098ce26d393191d5be37c8f7706a7aa1a3e6b52c1ab9d79bc1cd394e01`) pools the
  two body seeds per layout: the share of decisions towards cold while hungry and while fed, their difference with a
  95% interval from 10 000 bootstrap resamples of worms within each life (seed 92000), and the median peak
  temperatures.
- **Rules**:
    - **D0, the control**: each life's late energy repeats the control's greedy life (0.7408 and 0.6987 in 75000,
      0.6482 and 0.5899 in 23000).
    - **D1**: in 75000, the share of steering decisions towards cold (towards the food band) is lower while hungry than
      while fed, with the interval of the difference below zero.
    - **D2**: in 75000, the median temperature of the hungry value peak is above 20 °C (the warm half) and that of the
      fed value peak below it.
    - Descriptive: 23000, where hungry-only steering did not harm (+0.12 over plain, § Steering Only When Hungry).
- **What each outcome would mean**: D1 and D2 holding make the harm a matter of where hunger points the map's peak,
  and suggest steering by the hungry map's global contrast instead of its local slope. D1 failing refutes the hunch:
  hungry steering points to the food no less than fed steering, and the harm lies elsewhere - in how often the worm
  steers, or what steering does to its path.

### Results

Run on 2026-10-28 after the pre-registration was pushed; it waited for the composition sweeps and ran from 20:46 to
20:51 UTC. Commands as registered; `dirstats.py` as
`python3 -I <scratchpad>/dir/dirstats.py <dir.npz> 75000:-1 75777 75778` and `23000:-1 23777 23778`.

- **D0 holds**: the four lives repeat the control's greedy lives bit for bit, all 100 late energies of each (means
  0.7408 and 0.6987 in 75000, 0.6482 and 0.5899 in 23000).
- **D1 holds**: in 75000, hungry steering decisions point towards cold **0.483** of the time
  (n 335 456 decisions), against **0.572** while fed (n 980 617); the difference is −0.089, 95% interval −0.119 to
  −0.068.
- **D2 fails**: the value peak does not move with hunger. Its median temperature is 16.41 °C at hunger 1 and 16.41 °C
  at hunger 0.2 - on the food band (16.5 °C) in both states. The hunch as written - hunger moves the map's peak to the
  warm half - is refuted.
- **Descriptive, 23000** (where hungry-only steering did not harm): towards cold 0.502 hungry and 0.520 fed (−0.018,
  interval −0.034 to −0.005), a much smaller gap; and the value peak sits at 22.3 °C in both states, on the warm half,
  away from the food band. Hungry worms are a minority of decisions in 75000 (25%) and about half in 23000.

So in 75000 hungry decisions point towards cold less often than fed ones - about even between colder and warmer,
against 57% towards cold when fed. Whether that is worse aim depends on where the worms stand (below). Post hoc, from
the same `dir.npz` (`peaks.py`, SHA-256 `726466cf4cee66300c276343fb4b3ffecdbe71cb15138bc170b86d1888c36b79`, run as
`python3 -I <scratchpad>/dir/peaks.py <dir.npz>`), the peak is spread more than its median says: in 75000 it lies in the band's range (15.9 to
17.1 °C) in 74% and 68% of worm-samples (seeds 75777, 75778) and above 20 °C in 25% and 29%, at hunger 1; at hunger
0.2 the shares are 74% and 68%, 25% and 30%. In 23000 it is above 20 °C in 59% and 63%. And hunger barely moves it:
the peak is the same concept at hunger 1 and 0.2 in 98.7% and 97.7% of samples in 75000, 99.3% and 96.8% in 23000.
So hunger does not move the map's peak, and the cause of the gap is not where the peak is. Two readings remain, and this run cannot tell them apart:

- **The rule climbs a local slope, not to the peak.** Greedy steering compares the two concepts neighbouring the
  current temperature; if the hungry landscape has a local rise away from the food between the worm and the band, the
  worm climbs it. The peak alone does not show the slope along the way.
- **Where hungry worms are.** "Towards cold" is towards the food only for a worm warmer than the band. A worm in the
  strip colder than the band (x below −35) that steers towards the food steers towards warm. If hungry worms are more
  often there - having passed the band - D1's gap could be correct steering scored as wrong. This run did not record
  position, so the share of each is not known.

Next: record each decision's temperature against the food band's and the sign of the hungry and fed value slopes at
that temperature, so that steering is scored towards the food rather than towards cold, and a local rise can be seen
where it lies.

## The Composition Law Where Both Levers Work

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

The OR law (journal of 2026-10-27, § A First Design Rule) has so far been tested with two strong levers in only one
world, the cold layout 75000. Its tests tonight in a warm world and with the turning rule's `tau` (§ The Composition
Law Beyond Its First World and Pair, on its own branch) passed only because one lever barely moved the score there,
where ignoring that lever does as well. This test asks for the case that matters: the same two levers, slow learning
(`eta`) and a weak nose (`g_odour`), in two fresh worlds where both should work. It also asks whether last night's
pattern - mild pairs doing better than the law - comes back.

- **The worlds**: of the fifteen layouts of 2026-10-26 (§ The Hand-Built Worm on Fifteen New Layouts), the cold and the
  warm one where e45 trailed the hand-built (two-gene) worm most: cold 82003 (0.6219 against 0.8742) and warm 82104 (0.7608 against
  0.8584). Chosen for that gap, so that both levers have room; whether each one alone moves the score is checked, not
  assumed.
- **The sweeps**: `sweep3.py` (SHA-256 `115f82e6dca7b50ae396c18f05c5b2c415e071a39ef067c2c932991009c682f1`) runs
  `sweep2.py`'s cell function unchanged on `cold_eo`'s grid - log10 `eta` {−2, −2.4, −2.8, −3.1623} × `g_odour`
  {1.7237, 1.2, 0.75, 0.3786} - in each layout: 50 worms, body seeds the layout's + 777, 48 h, late energy over h24-48.
  Run as `python3 -I <scratchpad>/sweep3/sweep3.py cold82003 <out.npz>` and `warm82104`, from the repository's root.
- **The test**: `law3.py` (SHA-256 `8626d46922cd8195f3b80cc42974baad623e32dd5552c3c72cd8ea0d4052a168`) is `law2.py`'s
  test (EMAX 0.90, nine interior cells, bootstrap of worms within every cell, 10 000 resamples) with two more
  comparisons:
    - the OR law's mean absolute error minus that of the better one-lever rule (predicting a cell by its row's edge
      alone, or by its column's; the better on the full data is fixed for every resample);
    - the OR law's mean signed error, predicted minus measured, over the four mild cells (1, 1), (1, 2), (2, 1) and
      (2, 2): negative when mild pairs beat the law.

  Seeds 93000 (cold 82003) and 93001 (warm 82104). As a reference, on last night's sweep (data already seen, smoke
  seed 1) it gives: OR law 0.0185, additive 0.0591, row edge 0.0693, OR minus row edge −0.0508 (−0.0606 to −0.0338),
  mild cells' signed error −0.0332 (−0.0531 to −0.0158).
- **The control**: `h0.py` (SHA-256 `8b3e6ccc86b277157a3ab04fb7806137c6c85e61c005929c187b07113d3d8745`) compares the
  corners with the lives of 2026-10-26, which used the same life: cell (0, 0) is e45 and cell (3, 3) the hand-built
  worm. Red-first, it is also run once against the wrong layout (82004 for the cold sweep), where it must report a
  difference.
- **Rules**, for each sweep:
    - **H0**: both corners equal the 2026-10-26 lives worm by worm.
    - **Informative only if** each lever alone moves the score by at least 0.05 along its edge (about five cells'
      standard error); if not, S2 to S4 for that sweep are reported as uninformative.
    - **S1**: the OR law's mean absolute error over the interior is at most 0.03.
    - **S2**: the OR law's error is below the additive rule's, with the interval of the difference below zero.
    - **S3**: the OR law's error is below the better one-lever rule's, with the interval of the difference below zero.
    - **S4, mild pairs beat the law**: the mild cells' mean signed error is negative, with its interval below zero.
- **What each outcome would mean**: S1 to S3 holding in both worlds make the law a tested tool for composing these two
  levers across worlds, not one world's curve fit; failing in one marks where it stops. S4 holding in both makes the
  mild-pair synergy a regular feature to explain, with a mechanism still to find (tonight's map-cleaning hunch was
  not supported); failing makes it a property of layout 75000.

## Hungry Steering, Scored Towards the Food

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

In layout 75000 hungry decisions point towards cold, the food band's side, less often than fed ones, while hunger
barely moves the map's peak - for about 70% of worm-samples on the band (§ Where Hungry Steering Points). Two readings were left. **Position**:
"towards cold" is towards the food only for a worm warmer than the band, so hungry worms lying colder than it would be
scored as wrong for steering right. **A local rise**: the greedy rule compares only the two concepts neighbouring the
current one, so it can climb a bump in the hungry map that leads away from the peak. This run scores every decision
both ways.

- **The lives**: as § Where Hungry Steering Points - gr-fec's `SteeringBrain`, `greedy`, ungated, fixed body, 100
  worms, 48 h - in layouts 75000 and 23000, body seeds + 777 and + 778.
- **The tool**: `dir2.py` (SHA-256 `9f8c21f8cb8be727b53932512051b6f88d12dced63553bc8ef46caf9fe0ecd62`) scores each
  steering decision over h24-48, split by hunger (reading at or above 0.3), three ways: *cold*, as before; *food*,
  towards the food band from the worm's true position (towards cold if it stands warmer than the band, x above −35,
  towards warm otherwise); and *peak*, towards the temperature of the concept the brain's own values rank highest at
  the hunger it read, from the current concept (a decision taken on the peak concept counts as away). It also counts
  decisions taken colder than the band. A worm on the band itself is scored by which side of its centre line it
  stands. For evaluation only; nothing reaches the brain. A smoke run of half an hour of life (not reported as a
  result) checked that it runs. Run as `python3 -I <scratchpad>/dir2/dir2.py <out.npz> 75000:-1:75777
  75000:-1:75778 23000:-1:23777 23000:-1:23778` from the repository's root.
- **Statistics**: `dir2stats.py` (SHA-256 `530f2268f4f8857e43d88f81403a61ce66b88001106d7d037db6ac4ecff91e67`), as
  `dirstats.py` for all three scores on the same resamples, seed 94000 (75000) and 94001 (23000).
- **Rules**:
    - **E0, the control**: each life's late energy repeats the earlier run, and the *cold* shares repeat
      `dirstats.py`'s (0.483 hungry, 0.572 fed in 75000).
    - **F1, position**: in 75000, scored towards the food, hungry steering is no worse than fed: the interval of the
      hungry-minus-fed difference includes zero or lies above it. If F1 holds, where hungry worms stand explains the
      gap in the *cold* score.
    - **F2, a local rise**: in 75000, hungry decisions point towards the peak less often than fed ones, with the
      interval of the difference below zero.
    - Descriptive: the shares taken colder than the band, and 23000.
- **What each outcome would mean**: F1 holding and F2 failing would place the harm not in where the worm steers but
  in what steering does - its rate, or its effect on the path - and clear the map. F1 failing and F2 holding would
  locate it in the greedy rule's local view of the hungry map, and suggest steering by the map's global contrast. Both
  failing leaves a gap neither reading explains.

## Three Levers at Once

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

The OR law has predicted pairs of two strong levers - slow learning (`eta`) and a weak nose (`g_odour`) - in three
worlds (§ The Composition Law Where Both Levers Work, on its own branch). A design rule worth having must also compose
more than two. This asks whether multiplying three levers' remaining failures predicts their triples. The third lever
is `roam`, the gene through which hunger and gut stretch act on roaming (roaming is divided by 1 + `roam`·hunger·(1 −
stretch), journal of 2026-10-26), the gene whose return cost the optimised worm most after those two (journal of
2026-10-26, § Why the Optimised Worm Wins: Its Genes Swapped Back One at a Time: −0.0146, interval −0.0363 to +0.0051,
not shown).

- **The sweep**: `sweep4.py` (SHA-256 `94e2efaaee63206330980fc7f212a4ba3e3b0becddf7d8e656844ed66b336adb`), a 3 × 3 × 3
  grid on e45's median genome: log10 `eta` {−2, −2.5811, −3.1623}, `g_odour` {1.7237, 1.0512, 0.3786} and `roam`
  {1.4672, 2.8774, 4.2875} - e45's value, the midpoint and the CMA-ES genome's - in cold layout 82003, the world
  where the pair's levers were strongest. Each cell is a life as `build.py` lives it: 50 worms, body seeds + 777, 48 h,
  late energy over h24-48. Run as `python3 -I <scratchpad>/sweep4/sweep4.py <out.npz>` from the repository's root.
- **The test**: `law4.py` (SHA-256 `4528d7c161a72dcdd0573d1c1d4bebc43f7a76a9a0ed9cc282fdc87fcbc64662`): with EMAX
  0.90 and each lever's remaining failure measured alone along its edge, the law predicts E = EMAX − (EMAX − E₀₀₀)·a·b·c.
  Over the 20 cells where two or three levers move, it compares the law's mean absolute error with that of adding the
  three gains and of the best rule that ignores one lever (the law on the other two, chosen on the full data and
  fixed); intervals from 10 000 bootstrap resamples of worms within every cell, seed 95000. Before this was written it
  was checked on two synthetic grids: on an exact OR grid its error is 0; on an additive grid adding wins (OR minus
  additive +0.0378).
- **Rules**:
    - **H0, the control**: cell (0, 0, 0) equals the 2026-10-26 life of e45 in 82003 and cell (2, 2, 0) that of the
      hand-built worm, worm by worm (`h0.py`'s comparison).
    - **Informative only if** `roam` alone moves the score by at least 0.05 along its edge; if not, R2 and R3 are
      reported as uninformative (R1 still stands, as a test with a weak third lever).
    - **R1**: the law's mean absolute error over the 20 cells is at most 0.03.
    - **R2**: below the additive rule's, with the interval of the difference below zero.
    - **R3**: below the best rule ignoring one lever, with the interval of the difference below zero.
- **What each outcome would mean**: R1 to R3 holding make the law a rule for three levers, which can be composed from
  single-lever measurements alone - seven lives (the base and two per lever) instead of 27. R1 holding with R3 failing would mean the third
  lever's effect is lost in composition (redundant with the pair). R1 failing marks a real three-way interaction.

## Where a Worm's Map Comes From: Its First Meals
