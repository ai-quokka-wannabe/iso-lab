STATE.md item 9(b) asks for the cold-band hunch to be tested on fresh layouts. The hunch: hunger-gated steering by
the map's global contrast, with the greedy rule's 0.02 margin, helps where the band is cold and not where it is warm.
It came from the journal of 2026-10-28 (§ The Global Rule Elsewhere, § The Margin Rule Elsewhere), was named after
the data were seen, and rests on two layouts of each temperature, two of them chosen for the greedy brain's bad
result. This tests it on layouts that played no part in naming it.

- **The worlds**: the ten banded layouts of 2026-10-26 (§ The Hand-Built Worm on Fifteen New Layouts), all of them,
  chosen before any of them is run with these brains. Cold side −1: seeds 82000 to 82004. Warm side +1: seeds 82100
  to 82104.
    - Each is run with one body seed, the layout's seed + 777, as on 2026-10-26.
    - 82003 and 82104 were used in tonight's composition sweeps, with other brains; none of the ten has been run
      with a hunger gate or the global rule.
    - The rich layouts are left for a later run: the margin rule left both rich layouts tested within a few
      thousandths of never steering (V2).
- **The arms**, 100 worms each, 48 h, late energy over h24-48, gr-fec's code unedited, the fixed body:
    - the margin rule: `glob4.py` (SHA-256 `faa11bd5254ea38c4899fccdeb7148ff222fe7043b0ed9950656b593edaf6071`), job
      `L:side:L+777`;

      job `fixed:plain:L:side:L+777`.

  As a control, `gate/gate.py` (SHA-256 `24531668cf25b1bcd927476ec8525d824642c2626a8517026679c661acf0790d`) is run
  once in 82000, and its life must equal `glob4.py`'s `:greedy` life there bit for bit.
- **Commands**, from the repository's root, in this order:

  ```sh
  python3 -I <s>/glob4/glob4.py <s>/fresh10/glob.json \
      82000:-1:82777 ... 82004:-1:82781 82100:1:82877 ... 82104:1:82881 (and each with :greedy)
  python3 -I <s>/steer/ctl.py <s>/fresh10/ctl.json fixed:plain:82000:-1:82777 ... fixed:plain:82104:1:82881
  ```

- **Rules**:

    - **F2**: cold minus warm is above zero, with the interval's lower bound above zero.
    - Descriptive: each layout's differences, how many layouts per band have an interval wholly above zero, and the

- **What each outcome would mean**:
    - F1 and F2 holding: the hunch survives a test on layouts it was not named from.
    - F1 holding and F2 failing: the rule helps in fresh cold layouts, but no more than in warm ones. That refutes the
      hunch as stated; the band's temperature would not be what matters.
    - F1 failing: the rule's gain in cold 75000 and 23000 does not carry to fresh cold layouts, and the hunch is
      refuted.
    - F0 failing invalidates the comparison until it is explained.
    - Five layouts a band, one body seed each: the two-stage interval is wide, and a failure to show is not a

### Results

Run on 2026-10-29 after the pre-registration was pushed (committed 21:23:39, pushed 21:23:41 UTC). `run.sh` (SHA-256
`77c15639c9e88389b40a43f2ebc06a8cfd87912b393ddec946bb97ab8aacb87a`) ran the three registered commands in order, from
21:23:49 to 21:55:17. The stats ran on the outputs as registered, seed 99800.

- **F0 holds**: `gate.py`'s life in 82000 equals `glob4.py`'s `:greedy` life there, worm for worm
  (`np.array_equal` True; late energy 0.5576).
- **F1 fails, narrowly**: in the cold band the margin rule's mean lead over greedy gating is +0.032. Its two-stage
  interval, −0.006 to +0.068, includes zero.
- **F2 fails, narrowly**: cold minus warm is +0.054, interval −0.002 to +0.114.
- **The warm band**, for the record: −0.022, interval −0.070 to +0.021.
- Descriptive, layout by layout, margin rule minus greedy gating (95% interval over worms):
    - cold: 82000 +0.037 (−0.031 to +0.107); 82001 +0.025 (+0.000 to +0.050); 82002 +0.047 (−0.018 to +0.114);
      82003 −0.024 (−0.066 to +0.019); 82004 +0.073 (+0.014 to +0.132). Two of five intervals are wholly above
      zero.
    - warm: 82100 −0.072 (−0.132 to −0.013); 82101 −0.075 (−0.136 to −0.014); 82102 +0.017 (−0.023 to +0.056);
      82103 −0.010 (−0.071 to +0.050); 82104 +0.029 (−0.016 to +0.073). None is wholly above zero; two are wholly
      below.
- Descriptive, against never steering:
    - The margin rule is below never steering in all five warm layouts, every interval wholly below zero (−0.075 to
      −0.129).
    - In the cold layouts it is below in two (82002 −0.066, 82004 −0.089), above in one (82001 +0.065), and not
      distinguishable from it in two (82000, 82003).
    - Greedy gating is also below never steering in eight of ten layouts, by the arms' means
      (`ctl.log`, `glob.log`).

By the registered rules the hunch is refuted (F1 fails): the margin rule's gain in cold 75000 and 23000 is not shown
to carry to fresh cold layouts. The point estimates lean the hunch's way - ahead in four of five cold layouts, and
cold ahead of warm by 0.054 - but both intervals reach just below zero. With five layouts a band and one body seed
each, a failure to show is not a showing of no effect, as the registration said. The firmer result is descriptive
and not what was tested: in these fresh layouts gated steering of either kind mostly ends below a worm that never
steers, and in every warm layout the margin rule does. So the global-contrast fix does not undo the hunger gate's
harm in general. What it would take to tell a small cold-band gain from none is more body seeds per layout, with the
statistics set before the data are seen. Without the margin, the global rule is not run here.
