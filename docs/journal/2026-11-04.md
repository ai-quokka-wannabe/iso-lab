## Does the Greedy Rule's Direction Fail? Each Steering Decision Scored

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

STATE.md item 9 (b) names the next step left by the journal of 2026-11-03, § Why Steering Trails Never Steering in Warm
Layouts. There, on five fresh warm layouts:

- turns towards the band, kept alone, raised late energy by +0.1233 (+0.0421 to +0.2160) against never steering;
- turns away from it, kept alone, cost;
- the graded gain's own decisions split about evenly between the two (descriptive).

The reading left open was that the map's direction is what fails. A toy world: none of this is evidence about
*C. elegans*.

**The question**: when H14's graded gain steers, which link of the greedy rule fails to point it at the band?

Each decision passes three links:

1. the map chooses a temperature direction, warmer or colder;
2. the lateral sense says which side is warmer;
3. a turn towards that side is taken as a turn towards the food.

**The scores**, read for evaluation only from the world's ground truth after the brain has acted. Nothing reaches the
brain. Each scored decision is one made at a 10-s decision tick with a non-zero raw command, by a worm off band food
and outside the band's x-range. That means x below 29, where the band is warmer, or above 41, where it is colder.

- `map`: the chosen direction points the band's way in temperature.
- `lat`: the brain's lateral sense matches the noise-free sign of left minus right temperature.
- `tturn`: the turn truly rotates the heading towards the band's side in temperature.
- `bear`: the turn reduces the angle between the heading and the bearing to the nearest band patch's centre.

**The hunches**, each with what would refute it:

- **H-map, the map's direction is what fails**, *a hunch*. The share s_map is near chance. Supported if s_map's
  interval lies wholly below 0.6. Refuted if it lies wholly above 0.6. Undecided otherwise.
- **H-sense, the lateral sense does not fail.** Supported if s_lat's interval's lower bound is at least 0.9.
  Otherwise not supported, and the sense shares the failure.
- **H-geo, temperature is a fair guide to the band.** s_geo is the share of the turns towards the band in temperature
  that also reduce the bearing error to the nearest patch. Supported if its lower bound is at least 0.7. Refuted if its
  upper bound is below 0.6. Undecided otherwise.

If H-map is supported, the cost of steering is in the map. If it is refuted, the failure lies in the sense or the
geometry, and H-sense and H-geo say which.

- **H-map is undecided.** The map's choice points the band's way in about half the scored decisions, but the interval
  reaches 0.6194, past the registered 0.6, so the hunch is neither supported nor refuted by its rule. Its lower bound,
  0.4237, does not exclude a map that points the wrong way more often than not.
- **H-sense is supported.** The lateral sense names the warmer side correctly in 98.6% of scored decisions, in every
  layout (0.984 to 0.988).
- **H-geo is supported.** Of the turns that truly head towards the band's temperature, 94.5% also head towards the
  nearest band patch. Per layout the figure runs from 0.896 to 0.977.

**Descriptive**, not registered as tests:

- The turn's own correctness follows the map's choice. The share of turns truly towards the band's side in temperature
  is 0.5119 (0.4235 to 0.6177), and towards the nearest patch 0.4950 (0.4134 to 0.5868), both within 0.02 of s_map.
  With the sense and the geometry nearly always right, that is what arithmetic requires.
- The map's share varies widely between layouts, from 0.265 (82606) to 0.895 (82607). It is above 0.6 in two
  of the ten (82607, 82609; the control life read 0.650) and below 0.45 in four (82600, 82601, 82603, 82606).
- Over h0-24 the map's share is lower by its point estimate, 0.4577 (0.3701 to 0.5485). Weighted by the gain, which is steering's actual
  size, h24-48 gives 0.4668 (0.3884 to 0.5546): by the point estimates, the decisions taken when hungriest point the
  band's way less often than the unweighted ones, though the intervals overlap.

**Reading.**

- Of the greedy rule's three links, two are shown not to fail: the lateral sense and temperature as a guide to the
  food. So whatever goes wrong with steering's direction in these layouts goes wrong in the map's choice of
  temperature direction. That follows by elimination from two supported hunches. H-map's own registered test is
  undecided.
- The map's choice is not shown to be better than chance (0.5 lies inside its interval), and it is not shown to be at
  chance either. Its spread over layouts is the larger fact: in some worlds the map points home most of the time, in
  others mostly away.
- The near-even split agrees with findings.md § D4's hungry map, which points home from little more than half of far
  concepts (journal of 2026-10-28, in 75000). It is a reading, not a test.
- What comes next, if the series continues: what makes the map point away in layouts like 82606. For example, the
  value of the smelly-food side read against the band's, per worm, which the scores here do not record.
- Ten layouts, one body seed each, a toy world.
