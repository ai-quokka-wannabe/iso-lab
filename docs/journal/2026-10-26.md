## The gr-fec Sandbox, Taken In

Written by the maintainer's session, at the maintainer's request; not by the night staff.

- **The change**: Matej's research sandbox gr-fec, which between 24 and 26 October grew, tested and evolved an
  embodied, senses-only worm-level mind in about twenty pre-registered experiments, is written down in full in
  [docs/research/gr-fec.md](../research/gr-fec.md) - its worlds, body, senses and brains version by version, the
  assays and how it scored them, every result with its numbers, its seventeen principles, what it refuted, the problems
  it flagged and three it did not, and what its decoder phase teaches about brains. Its ideas become thirty new hunches,
  [§ G of Matej's ideas and hunches](../hypotheses/matej-hunches.md#g-from-the-gr-fec-sandbox), added to the plan
  index as "to plan" and to STATE.md's item 6, which now counts 128 hunches with G last.
- **How the lab should read it**: gr-fec is a toy world and not peer-reviewed; every number in the note is gr-fec's
  own, a lead to reproduce on the Grid and never evidence. The literature it names is listed unverified.
- **The archive itself**: gr-fec's code, ledger, pre-registrations and results are kept in
  [reference/gr-fec/](../../reference/README.md#gr-fec), read-only and exempt from the lab's style and *Rust first*
  rules, so its runs can be repeated; one personal detail was removed from its handoff, and one log's progress-display
  carriage returns became line breaks. The markdown and link checks skip `reference/`.
- **What it offers the lab most directly**: a complete design for a first learning brain - value predicted over grown
  concepts, instinct in a separate pathway, learned trust in instinct, inner senses as gates, integrated noisy senses,
  value-guided steering - that sits close to H12's plain worm and H13's grind, and a list of the ways such a brain fails.
