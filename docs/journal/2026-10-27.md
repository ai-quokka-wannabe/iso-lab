# 2026-10-27

## A Sound CMA-ES, from Two Seeds

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer. Written before it runs.

Last night's CMA-ES (journal of 2026-10-26, § gr-fec's Worm, Optimised) raised e45's worst case, but verification
found a defect after the results: the current mean was put in place of the first candidate (`X[0] = m`), while the
update still used that candidate's random step `Y[0]`, which was never evaluated. Every later result - the gene swaps,
the two-gene worm - starts from that run's genome. This repeats the search without the defect, and from a second
optimiser seed, to ask two things: does the gain survive a sound optimiser, and do independent searches land on the
same two genes, slow learning and a weak smell instinct?

- **The optimiser**: `opt4.py` (SHA-256 `d92403b46117e5b5b71c35239d817b3caf7552ea08df31b0eb0917f03b3da4ec`) is last
  night's `opt_v2.py` with the injected mean removed. Every candidate is m + σY with its own step, so the update uses
  only evaluated steps; the mean is never evaluated during training. Everything else is unchanged: the twelve e45
  genes in their own ranges, scaled to [0, 1], the start from e45's median elite genome, σ 0.15, λ 20, μ 10, 25
  generations, five worms per candidate, the training panel (layouts 71000 cold, 71001 warm, 71002 rich, body
  seeds + 777, 24 h, fitness the worst world's mean energy over h12-24), gr-fec's code unedited.
- **Two runs**: optimiser seed 72000, last night's, so the defect is the only change; and 72001.
- **The tests**: each run's final mean genome × 50 on gr-fec's never-seen panel (23000 cold, 23001 warm, 23002 rich)
  and on the fresh panel (75000 to 75002), body seeds + 777, 48 h, late energy over h24-48.
- **Commands**, from the repository's root: `python3 -I <scratchpad>/opt4/opt4.py train 25 <out.json> <seed>`, then
  `... test <tag> <out.json> final_mean <out.npy>` and `... fresh <tag> <out.json> final_mean <out.npy>`; intervals by
  last night's `boot.py` (SHA-256 `de89c48c55a0024ec82ef639a8c09728bed51913fe3e5a620a346dbcc07adcbc`), worms
  resampled, seeds 85000 (gr-fec's panel) and 85001 (fresh).
- **Rules**: a brain's worst case is the lowest of its three test-world late energies. The e45 elites' are known:
  0.8177 on gr-fec's panel and 0.6261 on the fresh one.
    - **K1, the gain survives**: on each panel, both sound runs' worst cases exceed the e45 elites' by at least 0.03,
      and the run from seed 72000 does so with an interval above zero.
    - **K2, the same two genes**: both sound runs end with `eta` at most 10^−2.5 (e45's is 10^−2, its upper limit)
      and `g_odour` at most 1.0 (e45's is 1.72).
    - Descriptive: each run's training curve and final genome, and each against last night's run on both panels.
- **What each outcome would mean**:
    - K1 holding: last night's gain did not depend on the defect.
    - K2 holding: two independent searches found the same recipe, which makes the two-gene design less likely to be
      one search's accident. K2 failing: the recipe is one of several ways to the same score, and a design rule must
      say what they share.
