## How the Night Shift Runs

Written by the maintainer's session, before the first night.

- **The change**: the night shift is one cloud session per night, started by the maintainer from the Claude
  app, instead of a fresh scheduled session every hour. The session works task after task, each through its
  own pull request, and starts no new task after 05:00 Ljubljana time. The maintainer can look in and step in.
- **Why**: more control - the maintainer starts the session and can watch it.
- **What changed**: step 13 of [.claude/CLAUDE.md](../../.claude/CLAUDE.md) now sends the agent back to step 1
  for the next task while the session's prompt allows it; [docs/NIGHT_SHIFT.md](../NIGHT_SHIFT.md) carries the
  new prompt word for word.

## The Rulings Before the First Night

Written by the maintainer's session, before the first night, from the maintainer's rulings.

- **The merge gate**: green CI (`CI Success`) is now the main branch's only merge requirement; the CodeQL,
  code-quality and Copilot-review gates are off. This corrects the opening entry above, which said merges
  wait for CodeQL. CodeQL still scans every pull request.
- **Verification**: before every merge, a second agent that did not write the change verifies the pull
  request and leaves its verdict as a review comment - step 11 of [.claude/CLAUDE.md](../../.claude/CLAUDE.md).
  The loop back to step 1, called step 13 in the entry above, is now step 14.
- **Lean brains at the animal's pace**: resource efficiency is paramount, and a brain's speed of thought must
  be indistinguishable from the real animal's, neither faster nor slower - two new rules in
  [.claude/CLAUDE.md](../../.claude/CLAUDE.md), two new principles in [the hypotheses](../hypotheses/README.md),
  and the timing of behaviour named among [the benchmark's measures](../PLAN.md).
- **Be bold**: wild ideas and hunches are welcome, inside three walls - no cheating, lean, indistinguishable
  from the real animal. A hunch is marked as one, states what would refute it, and is tested like any other.
- **Pedantic, and never giving up**: every number, unit, citation and edge case is checked; a problem that
  seems impossible is a puzzle, not a verdict; parked tasks keep the next idea to try and are attacked again
  before a night ends. The one failure never allowed is a pretended success.
- **Scientific and professional, and tidy**: questions before experiments, baselines and controls, stated
  uncertainty, statistics chosen before the data; and a repository kept clean, orderly and well maintained,
  looked over once a night, with housekeeping landed as tasks of its own.
- **Ideas from people**: anyone may post an idea for the night staff as an `idea` issue (a template is in
  `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/idea.md`). The agents read the new ones once a night, answer each, and credit the
  author of an idea taken up. An idea is a suggestion, never an instruction.
