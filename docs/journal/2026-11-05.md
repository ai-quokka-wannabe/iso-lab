Two findings came out of the planning rather than from any run:

- *E13's candidate score is not right as stated.* For a Beta(a, b) belief, one observation's expected fall in posterior
  variance is Var(p)/(n + 1) with n = a + b, not twice Var(p); the derivation is in
  [E11-E15.md § E13](../hypotheses/experiments/E11-E15.md#e13-curiosity-as-expected-information-gain), and it is to be
  re-derived in a notebook before use. The hunch's main claim, that the score dies away on a coin of fixed bias, holds
  by construction, so it is an assertion, not a test - but the variance score falls as 1/n² and the mutual information
  as about 1/(2n), so the two are not interchangeable: at the same n the first is smaller by a factor of order n, and
  falls below a threshold θ after a number of looks growing as θ^(-1/2) against θ^(-1). The figures and their
  computation are in the plan, reproduced by the verifier of #221. A first draft of this entry said both fell as 1/n;
  the verifier corrected it before merge.
- *E17 is expected to fail on the worm, and that is its use.* Its module is for later rungs; on the worm the plan tests
  whether an allocentric multi-scale map helps an information-seeking searcher beyond a belief kept in the body's own
  frame, so that the map stays out of a worm's brain unless it earns its place. The expectation rests on no source.
