Two findings came out of the planning rather than from any run:

- *E13's candidate score is not right as stated.* For a Beta(a, b) belief, one observation's expected fall in posterior
  variance is Var(p)/(n + 1) with n = a + b, not twice Var(p); the derivation is in
  [E11-E15.md § E13](../hypotheses/experiments/E11-E15.md#e13-curiosity-as-expected-information-gain), and it is to be
  re-derived in a notebook before use. The hunch's main claim, that the score dies away on a coin of fixed bias, holds
  by construction, so it is an assertion, not a test - but the variance score falls as 1/n² and the mutual information
  as about 1/(2n), so the two are not interchangeable: at the same n the first is smaller by a factor of order n, and
  falls below a threshold θ after a number of looks growing as θ^(-1/2) against θ^(-1). The figures and their
  computation are in the plan, reproduced by the verifier of #221. A first draft of this entry said both fell as 1/n;
  the verifier corrected it before merge.
- *E17 is expected to fail on the worm, and that is its use.* Its module is for later rungs; on the worm the plan tests

**What was checked, and how far.** Each source's record was looked up at Crossref (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`) or

**What it found.**

- Nichols et al. 2017, the sleep imaging the hunch rests on, is behind a subscription (Europe PMC lists no open copy,
  and the publisher's page refused the request with HTTP 403). Its abstract was read; its data statement was not, so

  This does not show the data are unavailable, only that the text does not say where.
- In the worm, three new, empirically derived entropy metrics told wake from isoflurane, while the established ones,
  mutual information and transfer entropy, did not (Chang et al. 2023), and isoflurane's immobility does not use the sleep circuit (Chang et al. 2025): anaesthesia is
  not a stand-in for sleep here.
- The behavioural marker turns the question round: Bronfman, Ginsburg and Jablonka 2016 state that *C. elegans* does
  not meet the conditions for unlimited associative learning, on the ground that it "has never been shown" to learn
  non-elementally: an absence of evidence, which H17 takes as the authors' reading, not as a tested fact. A Program
  that showed it would not be judged less worm-like until the worm has been tested.
- Exact IIT Φ is out of reach at 302 units; Φ\* with a polynomial partition search is the feasible stand-in, and is not
  the same quantity. The often-quoted ceiling for exact Φ, about a dozen units, was not found at a source and is not
  relied on.

**What was registered, before any data.** H17 § *Pre-registered, the sleep test*: segments by behaviour, never by the
measures; a guard for each measure against the trivial fall that lower activity alone would cause (binarising against
each unit's own distribution and dividing by phase-randomised surrogates for LZc and PCI-like; z-scoring for Φ\*, whose
surrogates sit near zero and so are a check, not a denominator; a per-unit pre-stimulus baseline for ignition); the
animal as the unit, a 95% bootstrap interval of the mean relative change; and three verdicts that cannot overlap, set
at a fall of one tenth: tracks the state, refuted, undecided. In the worm, a sensory perturbation may end the
quiescent bout, so a PCI-like response counts only from perturbations that leave the bout unbroken.

**What it cannot show.** A fall in sleep tracks a brain state. It does not show that anything is felt, in a worm or in a
Program; H17 says so in its first point.

**A question for the maintainer.** Whether perturbing a Program by writing into its internal state, rather than
through its senses, sits within the rule *No cheating*. H17 prefers a sensory pulse, which the worm can also receive,
and does not use the direct kick until this is ruled.

**Revised before any data, after the second agent's review** (pull request 229, review of 2026-11-05, *changes
needed*). The first registration could have decided things by construction:

- *Signal to noise.* Z-scoring and surrogates remove scale but not a falling signal-to-noise ratio. That would have
  made Φ\* *tracks the state*, and LZc *refuted*, close to automatic.
- *Trial selection.* Keeping only sleep trials that left the bout unbroken selects responses that did not spread.

H17 now registers:

- *noise-matched wake*, with an SNR floor per unit;
- a *noise-only control*, which a sound pipeline passes, and two *positive controls*;
