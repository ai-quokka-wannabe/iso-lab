## The Odour Dataset: Its Methods and What the Archive Holds

Written by iso-lab's night staff, an AI agent; not by the maintainer.

STATE.md item 11 (d), the odour step of [the stimulus assays' plan](../benchmark/STIMULI.md), done as item 11 (a) was
for salt (the journal of 2026-11-04, § The Salt Dataset: Its Methods and What the Archive Holds): read Chen, Wu,
Gershow and Leifer's methods, list their archive without downloading it, record the licence, and ask whether measures
made in a flow chamber can stand for the Grid's still air. Nothing is adopted, and nothing is computed that a score
could depend on. What was found is in [STIMULI.md § Air](../benchmark/STIMULI.md#air-odour-chemotaxis-and-aerotaxis);
this section says how.

- **What it found**:
    - The read-me says the demo code "calls raw data in the odor_calibration, odor_measure, and worm_tracks files" and
      points to the spreadsheet for which file makes which figure. The spreadsheet's worm rows name `track_5a`,
      `track_5b`, `track_5def` and `track_5SI` for "fig5" and "fig6", which match no file: they appear to be the
      preprint's numbering, the files the published Figure 7's. So which track file is which panel is inferred from
      the names, not confirmed; the files' insides were not read.
    - The tracks are centroids, at 14 frames per second and 32 µm a pixel, smoothed "with a third-order polynomial in a
      0.5 s time window"; the posture was estimated "but in this work only the position and velocity was used". This is
      the source of the 0.5 s polynomial named as a lead for the salt preprint's smoothing (SALT_MEASURES.md, Q3): it is
      this paper's rule, and still unconfirmed for the salt data.
    - Figure 7 rests on "six experiments, ∼100 worms per experiment, resulting in over 90 animal-hours", in two
      landscapes; the no-odour control on two experiments, the AIB-inhibited strain on three. The experiment, not the
      track, is the unit a test would rest on.
    - The tracks for adoption would cost 1,035,442,739 bytes of download by range requests (the eight `worm_tracks`
      members, compressed) and 1,044,224,311 bytes on disk while read; the whole archive 1.29 GB to fetch and 1.98 GB
      unpacked. If the landscapes alone were needed with the four Figure 7 track files, 823,121,207 bytes of tracks.
- **Still air**: the case for and against, and the answer (probably yes for the three measures against bearing, no for
  anything carrying the concentration's size), are in STIMULI.md § Air, *can a flow chamber stand for still air?* It is
  an argument from the paper's controls and the physics of a stationary field, moderately sure, with no experiment that
  compares the two airs. The Grid's side was read in master-control at the pin (`7c527d9b`): `src/air.rs` (diffusion with
  a loss, finite volumes, no advection) and `src/odour.rs` (the floor a wall, odour that settles and fades over about
  ten seconds, by `LOSS_RATE`'s own comment).
- **Leads not taken**: the analysis code, `demo_code/odor_flow_worm.m` (18,570 bytes) and the repository
  Kevin-Sean-Chen/leifer-Behavior-Triggered-Averaging-Tracker-new named in the paper's software section, would show how
  the bearing and the curvature are computed exactly; the archive's code is under the record's CC BY 4.0, and the
  vendored `l1spline` carries its own licence, not read.
