## Methods (for reproduction)
- Codes: random (3,6)-regular n=200 with 4-cycles removed (`src/ldpc.py`); CCSDS TC (128,64) from AFF3CT `CCSDS_64_128.alist` (validated: rank 64, girth 6, QC 16×16 circulants, degrees 3/5, check degree 8); random lifts of its 4×8 protograph with 4-cycle repair (`experiments/e03_ccsds_lifts.py`).
- Decoder: sum-product (tanh rule), 50 iterations, syndrome stopping, all-zero codeword, BPSK/AWGN, R = 0.5 for Eb/N0.
- Curvatures (`src/curvature.py`): Ollivier–Ricci with t-step lazy walks (α = 0.5) via exact optimal transport (POT); Devriendt–Lambiotte resistance curvature. Baselines: closed non-backtracking walk counts of length 6–12 per node.
- Statistics: Spearman, rank-residual partial correlations, permutation p-values, leave-one-code-out prediction, split-half / Poisson reliability.

## Bugs found and fixed during the sprint
- alist parser ignored zero-padding (gave a wrong CCSDS matrix) — fixed with degree assertion.
- Random circulant shifts are almost never 4-cycle-free (0/300) — replaced with a repair loop.
- Three simultaneous simulations exceeded 8 GB RAM; one batch was killed (its 6 results kept as exploratory).
- The 5 dB per-block CCSDS run was stopped unfinished to free resources.
